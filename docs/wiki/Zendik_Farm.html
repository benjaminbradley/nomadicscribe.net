Contact Log

        • e-mailed a general query:

Hey, I've known about Zendik farm for several years. The first time I heard about it was in Dallas, TX when one of your guys was selling cds and handing out zines. I remember thinking about how cool it sounded back then, but since I was barely in high school, it sounded like an impossible dream. Well, here I am years later, independent and still looking for the same thing. My parter April and I will be traveling around the U.S. in 2006, visiting sustainable communities. I have read your website and it sounds like you do allow visitors, and I was hoping to get more info about that.

Would it be possible for the two of us to come by some time next year to see your place? We'd love to know what life is like at Zendik from a first-hand perspective.

Thanks, Benjamin Bradley --Benjamin 20:02, 4 Dec 2005 (PST)

Hey Ben,

We're not really much of a sustainable community, I'm afraid.. if that's what you're looking for, I don't think you'll find it here, and so far as this time, we aren't having couples visit. Also, we never seem to be able to plan too far in advance what's gonna work as far as timing!! :) If you'd like, call closer to when you'll actually be in the area and we'll see what life's like then...
