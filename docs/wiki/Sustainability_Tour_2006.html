Sustainability Tour 2006

From NomadicScribe

See Main Page for the top pages.

This trip is about a lot of things - getting out of the city, listening to the birds and the trees, re-discovering the wild (as in wilderness) within me, connecting with people, doing something new and unplanned, experiencing new places and new people, using my time however I choose, learning from people and their communities, preparing for the future. But most of all, it's about freedom. --Benjamin 08:09, 5 Oct 2005 (PDT)
