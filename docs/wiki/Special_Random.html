Sustainability Tour 2006

This wiki will be used to plan and journal the sustainability 2006 tour by Benjamin and April.

  • Planning Calendar
  • Places - Communities we will visit
  • Friends - Friends and Family we will visit along the way
  • Calendar - Rough timeline
  • Travel - modes of travel
  • Money - budgetary needs
  • Research - what do we hope to learn?
  • Gear - what to bring with us (see our stuff for what we've left behind)
  • Survival - what if the shit hits the fan while we're on the road?
  • Wishlist - stuff we'd love to have but can't afford right now
