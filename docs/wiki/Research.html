Research

From NomadicScribe


Of course our experience will be our greatest asset, but I would also like to interview participants to learn from them directly about their experiences.

[edit]

Individual

  • How long have you been here?
  • Why did you join?
  • What's the most difficult thing you've had to deal with?
  • What are the benefits?
  • How is life different from wherever you were before?
  • If you could change anything, what would it be?
[edit]

Integration

  • How integrated is the community with surrounding areas/communities?
    • Economically?
    • Socially?
    • In terms of current events, local news, etc.?
  • Mutual benefits w/ others in the area?
  • Relative isolation level?
  • Possibility of military defense?
  • Political issues?
